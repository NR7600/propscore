# Slipscore feature inventory

Research date: Saturday, November 21, 2026 (America/New_York).

This inventory lists only what could be verified from Slipscore’s own site, official marketing screenshots hosted on that site, Slipscore’s X account, and first-party pricing/FAQ copy. Competitor tools (props.fyi, GameDay, Viqtory / Bet IQ) describe L5/L10/L20/H2H widgets and defense-vs-position ranks; those are **not** treated as Slipscore features unless a Slipscore source shows the same thing.

Items that were looked for and could not be confirmed are marked **unverified**. Nothing below is invented as a Slipscore fact.

## Product positioning (verified)

- Marketing name: Slipscore. Site: slipscore.io. X: [@Slipscoreapp](https://x.com/Slipscoreapp) (joined September 2025; ~19K followers at research time).
- Tagline: “The fastest player prop research tool. Every prop. Every game. Ranked by trend strength.” Source: [slipscore.io](https://slipscore.io/).
- Research platform, **not** a picks service. FAQ: “Slipscore is a research platform, not a picks service. We provide the data, trends, and analytics so you can make your own confident decisions.” Source: [slipscore.io FAQ](https://slipscore.io/).
- Mobile-first web app, “optimized for iOS, Android, tablet, and desktop.” Marketing screenshots are a mobile Safari / in-browser view of slipscore.io, not a unique native-app chrome. **No official App Store or Google Play listing was found.** Native app: **unverified**.
- Age/legal framing is implied by the betting-research positioning; a specific 21+ badge was not captured on the public homepage.

## Sports covered (verified)

Homepage and pricing list **six sports**: NBA, NFL, NCAAF, MLB, WNBA, and NHL. Source: [slipscore.io](https://slipscore.io/).

The sports chip row in official screenshots shows NBA, MLB, WNBA, and NFL (NCAAF and NHL are in the copy but not visible in those stills). X posts in November 2026 promote NBA, NFL, and CFB (NCAAF) trend lists, including “College Football Top 20 TD Trends.”

## Board / feed layout (verified)

Sources: [feature-trends.png](https://slipscore.io/feature-trends.png), [newtrans.png](https://slipscore.io/newtrans.png), [tweet4.png](https://slipscore.io/tweets/tweet4.png).

- Header logo “SLIPSCORE” and a hamburger menu (menu contents **unverified**).
- Subhead: “Updated Daily • Ranked By Trend Strength.”
- Top tabs: **All Trends**, **Over Trends**, **Under Trends**.
- Sports chips (multi-sport switcher).
- Games chips for tonight’s slate (e.g. “BOS vs CHA”, “NOP vs UTA”), horizontally scrollable.
- Smart filters (checkboxes): **100% Trends**, **Longest Streaks**.
- Stat-category chips. NBA still: All Stats, Points, Rebounds, Assists, and a combo chip starting “Points + Re” (cut off; consistent with Points + Rebounds). MLB still: Home Runs, Total Bases, Hits, RBI, Runs, and a cut-off “Sto” (likely Stolen Bases).
- Search. Official stills show a search icon plus a **MIN ODDS** slider (example floor **-120**). A user screenshot on the same site shows the placeholder **“Search players…”**.
- Ranked cards in a vertical feed, numbered RANK 1, 2, 3…

## Card contents (verified)

From official stills ([feature-trends.png](https://slipscore.io/feature-trends.png), [newtrans.png](https://slipscore.io/newtrans.png)):

- Rank number.
- Player name.
- Matchup, written as “BOS vs CHA” or “PIT @ CHC” (home/away indicated by vs / @).
- Side badge: **Over** or **Under** (Over is an orange outline pill in the stills; Under is a contrasting pill in [tweet4.png](https://slipscore.io/tweets/tweet4.png)).
- Market line with American odds, e.g. “Points Over 25.5 (-115)”, “Hits Over 0.5 (-115)”, “Three Pointers Made O/U 1.5”. Half-lines (.5) are the displayed format.
- Hit-rate windows as counts, not as “L5/L10/L20” labels. Visible windows include **4/4 games 100%**, **5/5 games 100%**, and **8/8 games 100%**.
- Streak callouts in orange: **“9x game streak”**, **“5x home streak”**, **“2x away streak”**, **“8x game streak”**. A card can show a game streak and a home or away streak at the same time.
- Footer: green **VIEW GAME LOG** button and a **full history >** link.

A cropped user screenshot ([tweet2.png](https://slipscore.io/tweets/tweet2.png)) shows a slightly different card chrome: sport + team (“NBA • MIL”), “Three Pointers Made O/U 1.5”, a **“7 trends”** disclosure, and the sentence “Hit in 4 of his last 4 games 100%”. Whether “7 trends” is an expandable list of windows is **not fully visible** in the crop.

Public X lists from @Slipscoreapp (Nov 21, 2026, mirrored at [teapawt.org/Slipscoreapp](https://teapawt.org/Slipscoreapp)) use the same research language, including 20-game windows and venue windows:

- `#1 Bitadze U 7.5 Reb+Ast (20/20)`
- `#3 Wolf O 0.5 3PM (11 game)`
- `#4 A. Mitchell U 1.5 3PM (11 home)`
- `#8 A. Thompson O 0.5 Stl (14 away)`

So 20-game hit counts, plus streaks tagged as game / home / away, are part of how Slipscore talks about the same board.

**Not visible on Slipscore cards (unverified as Slipscore UI):** explicit “L5 / L10 / L20 / season / H2H” labels, a numeric proprietary “Slipscore” other than rank + “trend strength”, opponent defense-vs-position rank, matchup letter grades, minutes/TOI trend, rest/back-to-back, pace, or a “without teammate X” split.

## Ranking (verified)

FAQ, [slipscore.io](https://slipscore.io/): “Every player prop is ranked by **hit rate, streak strength, and home/away splits**. The strongest trends surface to the top of your feed.”

The exact weights, whether unders are ranked in the same list as overs in “All Trends”, and any other inputs (H2H, injuries, odds) are **unverified**.

An X reply from @Slipscoreapp (earlier research) described windows as “x # of games is just the window that a play has hit in,” and the ranking as ordering players with the most trends. The precise algorithm remains **unverified**.

## Player detail (verified)

Sources: [feature-boxscore.png](https://slipscore.io/feature-boxscore.png), [feature-injuries.png](https://slipscore.io/feature-injuries.png), [feature-news.png](https://slipscore.io/feature-news.png), [tweet2.png](https://slipscore.io/tweets/tweet2.png). Homepage: “Tap any player to see their full game log, hit-rate chart, and box score history.”

- Modal header: player name; prop written in words vs the line, e.g. “Home Runs vs 0.5 line”, “Points Rebounds vs 23.5 line”, “Points Rebounds Assists vs 26.5 line”, “Three Pointers Made vs 1.5 line”.
- Next game: “Next game: vs COL” or “@ WSH”.
- Close (X) control.
- **Bar chart of recent games**:
  - One bar per game, height scaled to the counting stat.
  - The **raw stat value** is printed on the bar (not just hit/miss).
  - **Green = over the line**, **red = under the line**.
  - Date under each bar; opponent under the date (`vs. SF`, `@ MIA`).
  - Legend: green “Over 0.5” / red “Under 0.5” (or 23.5, 26.5, 1.5, etc.).
  - The stills do **not** show a horizontal line drawn through the bars; the line is communicated by color + legend. A drawn line is **unverified**.
  - About 10 recent games are visible in the stills. Whether “full history” loads more is **unverified**.
- Tabs under the chart: **Box Score**, **Injuries**, **News**.

### Box Score tab (verified)

- Scrollable table of the same recent games, newest first.
- MLB columns visible: DATE, AB, H, R, RBI, HR, TB, 2B, 3B, SB, BB.
- NBA columns visible in [tweet2.png](https://slipscore.io/tweets/tweet2.png): DATE, MIN, PTS, REB, AST, FGM, 3PM, STL, BLK.
- NHL box-score columns: **unverified** (no official NHL detail still found).

### Injuries tab (verified)

- Team pills for **both teams in the matchup** (e.g. NY and WSH).
- Player list with a red **Out** badge.
- Copy: “Live injury statuses for both teams in every matchup”; “IL designations and injury details at a glance”; “Updated continuously.”
- Timestamp: “Updated 4 min ago.”
- Day-to-day / questionable badges are claimed in homepage copy (“who’s in, who’s out, and who’s day-to-day”) but the still only shows **Out**. Other status pills are therefore only partly verified.

### News tab (verified)

- Game-level news, not a generic league firehose. Example: OG Anunoby, “NYK - F”, Out badge, a paragraph that he will miss a second consecutive game (hamstring) and that Landry Shamet and Miles McBride are usage candidates.
- Homepage: “Injury context, lineup changes, and usage notes.”
- Timestamp: “Updated just now.”

This is **context that a teammate is out**, not a calculated “without player X” hit-rate split. A statistical without-teammate split is **unverified**.

## Filters, search, and sorting (verified)

From [slipscore.io](https://slipscore.io/) and the board stills:

- Filter by All Trends, Overs, or Unders.
- Smart filters: 100% trends, longest streaks, stat categories.
- Game and sport filters.
- Minimum odds slider (example floor -120).
- Search any player.
- Default sort is **trend strength** (rank). “Longest Streaks” is a smart filter; the exact sort key when it is on is **unverified** beyond the name.

## Lines and markets (verified in part)

- Displayed lines are sportsbook-style **half-lines** with **American odds**, e.g. 25.5 (-115), 0.5 (-115), 23.5, 26.5, 1.5, 7.5. Sources: official stills and @Slipscoreapp lists.
- Both **Over and Under** are first-class (tabs + badge + chart colors).
- Combo / alt-style counting stats appear in the product:
  - Points + Rebounds (still + “Points Rebounds vs 23.5 line”)
  - Points + Rebounds + Assists (PRA; “Points Rebounds Assists vs 26.5 line”)
  - Reb+Ast (X list)
  - 3PM, steals, rebounds, assists, points (X list and stills)
  - MLB counting stats as listed above
  - **NBA First Basket** (pinned and repeated @Slipscoreapp posts, e.g. Nov 17 and Nov 19, 2026)
  - **NFL / CFB touchdown and first-TD** trend lists (@Slipscoreapp, Nov 19–21, 2026)
- Specific book branding (PrizePicks, Underdog, a named sportsbook) as the **line source inside the app** is **unverified**. Official stills show American odds, which are sportsbook-style, not PrizePicks-style multipliers.
- Alternate-line shopping inside a card (a dropdown of many books/lines) is **unverified**; multiple standard lines may exist, but the UI for switching books was not visible.
- User tweets featured on slipscore.io include **PrizePicks / sportsbook SGP screenshots** of settled slips. Those screenshots are betting-app tickets, not Slipscore chrome, and are not evidence of a built-in slip builder.

## Pricing and distribution (verified)

- New-user homepage offer: **$3.99/week**, marked down from $5.99, “full access, cancel anytime.” Source: [slipscore.io](https://slipscore.io/).
- Plans on the same page: Weekly **$3.99/week**, Monthly **$19.99/month**, Annual **$99/year** (marketed as about $1.90/week and “save 68%”). Each plan lists “Full player trends & analytics” and all 6 sports.
- Promo codes appear on X (e.g. weekly-plan code “TREND” on Nov 21, 2026, via [teapawt.org/Slipscoreapp](https://teapawt.org/Slipscoreapp)). A user screenshot also mentions “Start for $1.”

## What this inventory deliberately does not claim

- We did not log in, so anything only visible to paid subscribers after `/login` is unknown and marked unverified rather than assumed.
- We did not treat Viqtory, props.fyi, or GameDay feature matrices as Slipscore documentation.
- We did not treat PrizePicks tickets posted by Slipscore users as Slipscore UI.
